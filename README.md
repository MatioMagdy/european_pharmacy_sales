 European Pharmacy Sales & Operations Analysis

 Executive Summary
This project presents an end-to-end data analytics solution for a European pharmacy network. By leveraging *MySQL* for data querying and *Power BI* for visual analytics, the goal is to transform raw operational data into actionable business insights—focusing on revenue streams, product profitability, and sales performance across time and store locations.


 Data Architecture & Tech Stack
- Database Engine:  MySQL Workbench (Relational Data Modeling & Advanced SQL Queries)
- Business Intelligence: Microsoft Power BI (DAX, Measures, Drill-through Analysis, Navigation Bookmarks)
- Data Source: Enterprise Pharmacy Dataset (Star Schema: fact_sales, dim_product, dim_pharmacy, dim_date)



Key Business Questions Addressed
1. Financial Performance: What are the total revenue, net margin  and cost distributions?
2. Product & Inventory Analytics: Which product categories generate the highest margins versus unit volume?
3. Operational Optimization: What are the temporal trends in transaction volumes across different pharmacy locations?


 Key Findings & Strategic Insights
- Revenue vs. Margin: Identified key high-margin product lines driving overall profitability despite moderate unit sales.
- Transaction Dynamics: Segmented store performance to pinpoint underperforming pharmacy locations requiring operational re-alignment.
